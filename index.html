About Me

Hi! I am an M.S. student in Biomedical Engineering at Duke University (Aug 2025 – May 2027), working in the Vision and Image Processing (VIP) Lab advised by Prof. Sina Farsiu.

Before Duke, I received my B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Shanghai for Science and Technology (2021 – 2025).

My research focuses on real-world image restoration, especially semi-supervised learning guided by no-reference image quality assessment. I am interested in how quality priors can replace or complement ground truth when clean references are scarce, with applications spanning natural and medical images.

I plan to pursue a Ph.D. starting Fall 2028 and am always happy to chat about low-level vision and medical imaging. For more details, please see my CV.
